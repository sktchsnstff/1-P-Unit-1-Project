My Favorite Videogames:

By Joao Machado

(Yes, I am a nerdy loser.)

General List:

  • Deltarune
  • Hollow Knight
  • Insomniac's Spider-Man
  • Cassette Beasts
  • FNAF
  • Rainworld

Tier List And Reasons:

  1. Hollow Knight - Incredibly beautiful artwork, with a massive and vivid world, full of mysteries, secrets, and enemies to cut down!
  2. Deltarune - Fun turn-based combat mixed in with a bullet hell, the story is goofy and silly, but also has its more serious moments, and delves a lot into escapism.
  3. Insomniac Spider-Man - Phenomenal graphics, intense combat, and interesting characters. I would put this higher, if it just wasn't for the fact that the other games are my hyper-fixations.
  4. Cassette Beasts - Takes the usual style of monster-catching games and inovates on it so much! The art and music are diverse and iconic, though it is fairly simple.
  5. Rainworld - I get to play as a cute little slug that looks like a cat, in a simulated ecosystem that is very realistic. It's peak, besides the fact that it's hard as HELL.
  6. FNAF - Used to be my favorite. Has some cool lore, until the 8th game, but I still like the concept.
